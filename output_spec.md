# Output Specification

## Shell Company Screening Report

The final report should present the research findings, candidate assessment, transaction feasibility analysis, and final prioritization.

### 1. Executive Summary

Provide a concise overview of:

- the research objective;
- the client mandate and transaction context;
- the research scope;
- key findings;
- recommended candidates or candidate groups; and
- major unresolved issues.

The summary should highlight the overall transaction assessment rather than only describe individual companies.

### 2. Candidate Universe and Screening Results

Present the candidate discovery and screening outcomes.

Include:

- the number of candidates identified;
- screening methodology;
- hard-filter results; and
- key reasons for candidate exclusion or progression.

The screening results should clearly indicate:

- `PASS`
- `PASS WITH CAVEAT`
- `FAIL`

for evaluated candidates.

### 3. Candidate Comparison

Provide a comparative overview of candidates that proceeded to deeper analysis.

Compare candidates based on factors including:

- ownership and control feasibility;
- dealability;
- capital structure;
- regulatory and legal condition;
- restructuring burden;
- platform suitability; and
- transaction feasibility.

### 4. Detailed Candidate Analysis

For each shortlisted candidate, provide structured analysis covering:

#### Company Overview

- business background;
- listing information;
- current operating condition; and
- relevant background information.

#### Ownership and Dealability

- major shareholders;
- ownership structure;
- potential control path;
- shareholder-related considerations; and
- unresolved dealability issues.

#### Financial and Structural Assessment

- financial condition;
- liabilities;
- capital structure;
- dilution considerations; and
- legacy issues affecting transaction execution.

#### Regulatory and Compliance Assessment

- reporting status;
- audit condition;
- litigation and regulatory issues;
- listing-related concerns; and
- other material compliance factors.

### 5. Transaction Feasibility Assessment

Assess whether the candidate can realistically support the proposed transaction.

Include:

- control acquisition path;
- regulatory considerations;
- restructuring requirements;
- post-acquisition platform use;
- transaction economics;
- key assumptions; and
- execution risks.

Where exact transaction terms cannot be verified, describe the relevant drivers and uncertainties instead of providing unsupported estimates.

### 6. Candidate Prioritization and Recommendation

Provide the final assessment of candidate suitability.

Include:

- prioritized candidate list or candidate grouping;
- reasons for prioritization;
- major advantages;
- key risks;
- unresolved issues; and
- recommended next actions.

Prioritization should consider:

- dealability;
- execution certainty;
- regulatory feasibility;
- restructuring burden;
- platform fit; and
- transaction economics.

### 7. Sources and Supporting Evidence

Provide references for material factual information used in the report.

Sources should prioritize authoritative public disclosures according to the source priority defined in the agent instructions.
